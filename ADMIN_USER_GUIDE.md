# HIREMIND AI — Non-Technical Administrator User Guide

Welcome to **HIREMIND AI**. This guide is designed for recruiters, hiring managers, educators, and administrators who want to customize and manage the platform without technical, programming, or database knowledge.

---

## 1. Quick Start: Managing Your Platform

### Step 1: Open the Control Center
- Click the **Gear icon (Control Center)** in the top-right corner of the screen.
- Or use the keyboard shortcut: press `Ctrl + /` (or `Cmd + /` on Mac) and select **"Open Control Center"**.

### Step 2: Sign in as Administrator
- The first user who creates an account on the platform automatically receives **Administrator** permissions.
- You can also sign in with any account registered with an administrator email address.

---

## 2. Common Customization Tasks

### Changing the Brand Name and Tagline
1. In the Control Center, open the **Brand** tab.
2. Enter your new **Product Name** (e.g., *CampusRecruit AI* or *TechPrep AI*).
3. Enter your **Tagline** (e.g., *AI-Driven Student Interview Readiness*).
4. Check the **Live Preview** on the right side to see how your header and cards will look.
5. Click **Save Changes**. All users will instantly see the new branding.

### Changing the Color Theme
1. In the Control Center, open the **Appearance** tab.
2. Click on any color swatch:
   - **Ocean Blue** (Clean & Professional)
   - **Deep Indigo** (Enterprise Grade)
   - **Royal Violet** (Modern Creative)
   - **Emerald Green** (Growth & Success)
   - **Amber Warm** (Energetic & Inviting)
   - **Rose Crimson** (Dynamic & Bold)
3. Preview the buttons and score rings in the live preview.
4. Click **Save Changes**. The theme changes across the entire platform immediately.

### Adjusting Interview Difficulty
1. Open the **Interview** tab.
2. Choose your default difficulty:
   - **Auto**: Automatically matches the complexity of the candidate's skill gaps.
   - **Easy / Medium / Hard**: Forces questions of a specific depth level.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

### Changing Scoring Weights
1. Open the **Scoring** tab.
2. Adjust the sliders for each assessment factor:
   - **Required-Skill Alignment**: How much emphasis to place on mandatory job requirements.
   - **Evidence Strength**: How deeply evidenced the candidate's experience is.
   - **Semantic Relevance**: How closely candidate skills match job terms.
   - **Coverage Breadth**: Total number of matched competencies.
3. Note: The weights must always total **100%**. If the sliders do not equal 100%, the platform will clearly highlight the difference.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

### Adding Custom Job Roles & Skill Categories
1. Open the **Skills** or **Roles** tab.
2. Click **Add New Skill** or **Create Role**.
3. Type the title, description, and required skills.
4. Click **Save**. The role and skill immediately appear in candidate matching.

---

## 3. Monitoring System Health

1. Open the **Overview** tab in the Control Center.
2. Inspect the **System Status**:
   - 🟢 **Healthy**: Everything is running at peak performance.
   - 🟡 **Degraded**: Built-in deterministic backup engine is active while AI reconnects.
   - 🔴 **Unavailable**: Issue detected with network or database connection.
3. Review total sessions analyzed, active users, and system latency.

---

## 4. Safety & Recovery

- **Reset to Defaults**: Every setting tab has a **"Reset to Factory Defaults"** button. Clicking this restores default HireMind settings without deleting candidate resumes, interview histories, or user accounts.
- **Export Data**: The **Data** tab lets you download a full JSON backup of all sessions anytime.
