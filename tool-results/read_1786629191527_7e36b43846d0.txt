     1→     1→# HIREMIND AI — APPLE-INSPIRED PREMIUM UI/UX SYSTEM
     2→     2→
     3→     3→Redesign the entire HireMind AI frontend with a premium design philosophy inspired by the qualities of Apple's software design:
     4→     4→
     5→     5→**Clean. Sleek. Minimal. Intuitive. Premium. Human.**
     6→     6→
     7→     7→Do NOT copy Apple's proprietary interface, layouts, icons, branding, typography, or visual assets.
     8→     8→
     9→     9→Instead, adopt the underlying design principles:
    10→    10→
    11→    11→> "Complex technology should feel simple to the person using it."
    12→    12→
    13→    13→HireMind AI must look like a product designed by a world-class product design team, not a typical hackathon dashboard.
    14→    14→
    15→    15→==================================================
    16→    16→
    17→    17→## 1. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
    18→    18→
    19→    19→==================================================
    20→    20→
    21→    21→The primary design principles are:
    22→    22→
    23→    23→### 01 — CLEAN
    24→    24→
    25→    25→Remove anything that does not help the user understand or complete the task.
    26→    26→
    27→    27→Every screen should have one primary purpose.
    28→    28→
    29→    29→Avoid:
    30→    30→
    31→    31→• unnecessary cards
    32→    32→• excessive borders
    33→    33→• excessive badges
    34→    34→• decorative charts
    35→    35→• redundant information
    36→    36→• unnecessary buttons
    37→    37→• visual clutter
    38→    38→
    39→    39→Use whitespace intentionally.
    40→    40→
    41→    41→---
    42→    42→
    43→    43→### 02 — SLEEK
    44→    44→
    45→    45→The interface should feel refined and precise.
    46→    46→
    47→    47→Use:
    48→    48→
    49→    49→• soft surfaces
    50→    50→• subtle depth
    51→    51→• precise spacing
    52→    52→• smooth transitions
    53→    53→• restrained shadows
    54→    54→• elegant typography
    55→    55→• carefully balanced layouts
    56→    56→
    57→    57→Nothing should feel visually heavy.
    58→    58→
    59→    59→---
    60→    60→
    61→    61→### 03 — PREMIUM
    62→    62→
    63→    63→The product should feel expensive and trustworthy.
    64→    64→
    65→    65→Premium does NOT mean adding more visual effects.
    66→    66→
    67→    67→Premium means:
    68→    68→
    69→    69→• excellent spacing
    70→    70→• excellent typography
    71→    71→• consistent components
    72→    72→• meaningful animation
    73→    73→• visual hierarchy
    74→    74→• predictable interaction
    75→    75→• attention to small details
    76→    76→
    77→    77→---
    78→    78→
    79→    79→### 04 — SIMPLE
    80→    80→
    81→    81→A first-time user should understand the product without documentation.
    82→    82→
    83→    83→The user should immediately understand:
    84→    84→
    85→    85→"What do I do?"
    86→    86→
    87→    87→"What did HireMind discover?"
    88→    88→
    89→    89→"What should I do next?"
    90→    90→
    91→    91→---
    92→    92→
    93→    93→### 05 — HUMAN
    94→    94→
    95→    95→AI should feel like an intelligent assistant, not a machine control panel.
    96→    96→
    97→    97→Use natural language.
    98→    98→
    99→    99→Instead of:
   100→   100→
   101→   101→"SEMANTIC_SCORE = 0.82"
   102→   102→
   103→   103→Show:
   104→   104→
   105→   105→"Strong alignment with the target role."
   106→   106→
   107→   107→Instead of:
   108→   108→
   109→   109→"GAP_VECTOR: 0.41"
   110→   110→
   111→   111→Show:
   112→   112→
   113→   113→"System Design is your biggest current gap."
   114→   114→
   115→   115→Instead of:
   116→   116→
   117→   117→"MODEL_OUTPUT"
   118→   118→
   119→   119→Show:
   120→   120→
   121→   121→"Why we recommend this question"
   122→   122→
   123→   123→==================================================
   124→   124→

   139→   139→Primary text:
   140→   140→
   141→   141→Deep charcoal / near-black.
   142→   142→
   143→   143→Secondary text:
   144→   144→
   145→   145→Muted gray.
   146→   146→
   147→   147→Accent:
   148→   148→
   149→   149→A restrained blue/indigo used only for important interactive elements.
   150→   150→
   151→   151→Success:
   152→   152→
   153→   153→Soft green.
   154→   154→
   155→   155→Warning:
   156→   156→
   157→   157→Soft amber.
   158→   158→
   159→   159→Critical:
   160→   160→
   161→   161→Soft red.
   162→   162→
   163→   163→Do not make the entire interface colorful.
   164→   164→
   165→   165→Color should communicate meaning.
   166→   166→
   167→   167→==================================================

   225→   225→
   226→   226→BODY

   228→   228→Comfortable
   229→   229→Readable

   231→   231→
   232→   232→METADATA

   234→   234→Small
   235→   235→Muted

   237→   237→Avoid excessive bold text.
