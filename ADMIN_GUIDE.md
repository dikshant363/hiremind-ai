# HireMind AI — Administrator & Operations Guide

## 1. Accessing the Control Center
1. Navigate to the top navigation header and click the **Control Center** (Sliders icon) or press `Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K` and search "Control Center".
2. If not signed in as an administrator, the Control Center will be in **Read-Only Mode**.
3. Sign in with an admin account or register the first account on a fresh database to obtain admin privileges.

## 2. Managing System Settings
- **Brand & Style**: Modify application name, tagline, and choose accent color theme.
- **Scoring Formulas**: Adjust the 4-factor Match formula weights and 5-factor Readiness formula weights with automatic 100% sum validation.
- **System Health**: Click "Ping Health" to trigger live diagnostics across SQLite database latency, AI engine, and PDF/DOCX extractors.
- **Audit Trail**: Inspect recent administrative actions, logins, and errors with timestamps and user emails.
- **Factory Reset**: Click "Reset Defaults" in the footer to restore default configurations.
