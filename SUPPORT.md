We are here to help you get started, troubleshoot issues, and contribute to HireMind AI.

## 🔍 Where to Look First

1. **Documentation:**
   - [README.md](README.md) — Overview, features, and quickstart guide
   - [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md) — Step-by-step Vercel + Neon deployment instructions

   - [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) — Guide to running all unit, integration, and red-team QA suites

2. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):**
   - **Why does the system say `deterministic-fallback`?**  
     If no valid Google Gemini API key is configured in your `.env`, HireMind AI automatically falls back to its built-in deterministic intelligence engine. The app remains 100% functional offline!
   - **How do I deploy on Vercel for free?**  
     Follow the [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md) guide using Vercel Hobby + Neon PostgreSQL serverless pooling.
